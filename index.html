Who we are

A cleaner ride for every journey

Zproo (ZPROO EV Private Limited) started in 2025 in Hinjawadi, Pune with a simple belief: getting around your city shouldn't cost the planet. We run a fully electric ride network, so every trip booked on Zproo is a zero-emission trip.

We're still early, which means the people who join now shape the product, the culture and the way we grow. If you want real ownership and fast learning, this is the place.

Questions?

Frequently asked

How do I apply?

Click "Apply" on any role and fill in the short form. It opens an email to career@zproo.com with your details. Attach your résumé and hit send. You can also email us directly.

Where is Zproo based?

Our office is in Hinjawadi, Pune, Maharashtra. Most roles are on-site or hybrid, since we work closely with our on-ground operations.

Do you hire freshers and interns?

Yes. We look for curiosity and ownership, not just years of experience. Check the internship listings or send a general application.

How long does the process take?

Usually 2–3 weeks from application to offer. We'll keep you updated at every step.

I want to become a Zproo driver partner. Is this the right page?

This page is for team roles. For driver partnerships, email career@zproo.com with the subject "Driver Partner Enquiry" and our operations team will reach out.
